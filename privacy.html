German Snacks

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version: German Snacks collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send anything to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, the app keeps on your device which words and grammar rules you have practised, your scores and records, your daily goal and streak, your chosen level, and your settings.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of that same progress in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to your other devices. This copy is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it or access it.

Backups

If you use Save backup, the app creates a file with your progress and lets you choose where to put it. Where it goes is up to you.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, name, email or account of any kind.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking tools.
  • No access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.
  • All voices are recorded in advance and built into the app. Nothing you type or say is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Erase progress to delete your progress on this device and in your iCloud copy. Deleting the app also deletes everything stored on the device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
