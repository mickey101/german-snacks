# German Snacks – noun data

`german-nouns.csv` lists the 2,991 German nouns used in the German Snacks app, with their article (der, die, das) and plural.

## Where it comes from

This list is based on **german-nouns** by gambolputty (https://github.com/gambolputty/german-nouns), which was compiled from the **German Wiktionary** and its contributors (https://de.wiktionary.org).

For German Snacks, the list was filtered to the nouns used in the app, and genders and plurals were checked and corrected. Plurals that learners would not use (for example for uncountable words like *Milch* or *Musik*) were left out.
