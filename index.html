Support

Questions, bugs, ideas?

Email snacksgerman@gmail.com – screenshots help!

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

If you use iCloud on both devices, progress follows you automatically. You can also use Settings › Backup › Save backup, then Load backup on the new device.

I already know some German. Do I have to start from the beginning?

No. Choose your level in Settings › Level. Everything below it counts as known and only comes back now and then for a quick check.

How do I start over?

Settings › Erase progress. This can't be undone, so save a backup first if you might want it back.

Where do the words and voices come from?

See Settings › Credits & sources in the app. The noun list with articles and plurals is free to download: german-nouns.csv (CC BY-SA 4.0).
